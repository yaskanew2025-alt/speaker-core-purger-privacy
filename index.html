Privacy Policy

App: Speaker Core Purger (package com.speakercorepurger.app)
Developer: Yasin Abdulaziz Mohamed
Last updated: 5 October 2026

Speaker Core Purger plays sound and gentle vibration to help clear water and dust from a phone speaker. This policy explains what information the app uses and what it does not.

1. Summary

  • No account, sign-in or registration is needed.
  • The app does not record audio and does not use the microphone, camera, contacts, photos, files or location permission.
  • Your settings and purge history stay on your device and are not sent to us.
  • The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain device information, described below.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves your chosen theme, mode, duration, custom frequency, vibration and volume options, and a list of your recent purge sessions (mode, time, duration and whether it completed). This data is stored only on your phone. We do not receive it and cannot see it. You can clear the history inside the app (History > Clear), or remove everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

3. Permissions the app uses

PermissionWhy
Internet and network stateTo load and show ads.
VibrateTo vibrate gently during a purge if you turn vibration on.

While purging, the app can raise the media volume to maximum and restores it afterwards. It plays sound through the phone speaker only.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob to show banner, interstitial and rewarded ads. AdMob and Google may collect and use the following to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and improve their services:

  • Advertising ID and similar device identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (such as device model, operating system version, app version and crash or performance diagnostics)
  • Information about how you interact with ads

We do not receive this information and do not combine it with any other data. Google's use of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions where it is required, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, using Google's consent tool. You can review or change your choice at any time in the app under Settings > Privacy and ad choices. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your phone's system settings (usually Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal data and do not run our own servers that collect user data. The only third party involved is Google, through AdMob, as described above.

6. Children

The app is a general utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security and retention

Because the app keeps its data on your device, you control how long it is kept. Data handled by AdMob is kept according to Google's own policies.

8. Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. To exercise rights over data held by Google, use your Google account's privacy controls at myaccount.google.com.

9. Safety notice

Keep the phone away from your ears while the app plays sound, and stop if you hear crackling or distortion. The app cannot repair a damaged speaker.
